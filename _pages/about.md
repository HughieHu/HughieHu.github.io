I'm a senior undergraduate at [NYU Shanghai](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/) majoring in Computer Science. My research interests include **AI for Scientific Research**, **Deep Research Agents**, and **Information Retrieval**.

My research focuses on search agents that retrieve and reason over scientific literature: evaluating existing systems ([SciArena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01001)), improving retrieval for deep research agents ([SAGE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05975)), and training page-native agents that read documents directly as page images instead of OCR text ([DR Pixel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03097)). I am also interested in making agentic systems reliable in high-stakes domains such as finance ([FinTrust](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.512/), [FinLFQA](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.908/)).

I am fortunate to work with Prof. [Chen Zhao](https://henryzhao5852.github.io) (NYU Shanghai), Prof. [Arman Cohan](https://armancohan.com/) (Yale University), Prof. [Sewon Min](https://www.sewonmin.com/) (UC Berkeley), and [Yilun Zhao](https://yilunzhao.github.io/) (Yale University). In Summer 2026, I was a visiting student researcher at [Berkeley NLP](https://nlp.cs.berkeley.edu/), advised by Prof. Sewon Min.
